# PRE-REGISTRATION R2B -- a mesh-independent scale on the paper's three-dimensional transformer (S on the Phase-2b label-free transformer)

**Branch:** wp9-pool. **Status:** r2, stamped 3 November 2026 before any run: the two CONFIG_SHA256 lines below are filled, and the last line is this file's SHA-256 (Sec. 7); post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** every outcome below is publishable; any write-up reports R2b's verdict with each condition's rel, tau and per-seed values, the fine energy gap and von Mises error beside it (Sec. 6), and R2a's verdict beside R2b's.

## 1. Question
In three dimensions the label-free (AR) transformer of the Phase-2b run loses accuracy on the fine mesh it never trained on: on P3's fine evaluation set (lc 0.0374) its displacement error is 8.62 times its in-band error (seed means 0.258 and 0.0300; the Phase-2b report's KP4). wp8's post-hoc reading 4e found an amplitude deficit in it: its median c* (the energy-optimal amplitude of each load case, label-free; "median" as in PREREG_FINE3D, the mean over seeds of each seed's median) is 1.23 on the fine set against 1.00 in band; scaling each load case by its c* lowers the fine displacement error by 68% (and the in-band one by 53%); on 16 geometries meshed again at the in-band ends and middle and at the fine lc, its c_b rises from 0.98 at lc 0.0906 to 1.27 at 0.0374, and its predicted energy norm falls to 0.75 of its coarsest-mesh value. PREREG_FINE3D's R2a (SUPPORTED, adjudicated 21 October 2026) tested S -- a decode scale and load inputs that do not change with the mesh -- on the token bottleneck at M = 1,024 tokens, against a fresh baseline: S lowered the fine displacement error by 94.0% (0.513 to 0.0310; tau 13.7%), the fine / in-band ratio by 75.9% (4.96 to 1.19; tau 29.4%), and the in-band energy gap and displacement error by 72.3% and 75.8%; its median c* on the fine set was 0.99 (the fresh baseline's 1.81), and on the remesh geometries its c_b stayed between 1.00 and 0.99 and its predicted energy norm between 1.00 and 1.02 (the fresh baseline's: c_b 0.93 to 2.02, the energy norm down to 0.47). By PREREG_FINE3D's R2b rule, fixed before R2a ran, R2b was recommended (S's fine rel -93.96% <= -45%); the PI decided on 21 October 2026 that R2b is run, and on 3 November 2026 to prepare it.
- **Q-R2b.** Does S reduce the growth of the displacement error from the training mesh sizes (lc 0.0579-0.0906) to the fine mesh for the paper's transformer, the Phase-2b label-free arm, as it did for the token bottleneck? Asked of the transformer with and without S, both trained afresh on seeds 3-5 in one session.

## 2. Arms
| Configuration | Seeds | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `configs/r2b_b.json` | 3-5 | The Phase-2b label-free (AR) transformer trained again on fresh seeds (`e8.seed_offset = 3`): S's reference. |
| `configs/r2b_s.json` | 3-5 | As `r2b_b` with S, R2a's: `model.decode_scale = "l1"` with `model.decode_scale_factor = 2^-10` (the decoded field is multiplied by 2^-10 times the sum of the absolute values of the battery's nodal force components instead of by the largest of them) and `model.features.load_density = true` (per-node load densities -- each nodal force divided by the boundary area (traction) or the volume (body load) the node carries -- and a load summary of mesh-free ratios). |

Both: everything else is the Phase-2b configuration (`configs/phase2b_v1.json`, CONFIG_SHA256 `316f5e6e...` in PREREG_PHASE2B) in the AR-only form of PREREG_E2 -- E8 AR only, P3 zero-shot only (no labels bought), the FE-JEPA-only probes e6 and wp6 disabled: the corpus `runs/data3d_phase2` (manifest `dc75628290bb...`) and its fine set `runs/data3d_phase2_fine` (`0f859ad98a01...`), split 256 / seed 1, the 1,024-instance training prefix, 200 epochs at lr 1e-3 (204,800 AR steps per seed), dims 256 / 8 / 8, TF32 with fp32 weights, one worker. The configurations are generated (`scripts/make_r2b_configs.py`, which refuses the Phase-2b file unless its canonical SHA-256 is PREREG_PHASE2B's stamped line, and imports E2's AR-only edit and R2a's factor instead of copying them); a test regenerates them byte for byte and checks that each differs from the Phase-2b configuration only in `_comment`, `out`, `prereg_file`, `e8.seed_offset`, the AR-only keys (`e8.ar_only`, `e6.enabled`, `wp6.enabled`, `p3_transfer.fewshot_budgets`, `p3_transfer.naive_budget`) and, for S, its three model keys, and from R2a's configuration of the same role (`configs/r2a_b_m1024.json`, `configs/r2a_s_m1024.json`) only in the bottleneck's three model keys (`kind`, `n_tokens`, `decode_k`), `_comment`, `out` and `prereg_file`. The top-level `seeds` key stays as it is: the runner reads the seeds from `experiments.e8`.

Why a fresh baseline on fresh seeds: the Phase-2b states were read (wp8 4e; fine3d's R3 started from them) and their fine-mesh deficit is why S exists; comparing S with them would select on their training noise (PREREG_W9 Sec. 2's argument, as for R2a). Both arms then also share the commit and the runtime (torch, GPU and TF32 setting, which the adjudication compares) and run in one session, each step's start recording the host and GPU driver it starts on (Sec. 7); and the design is the one PREREG_FINE3D simulated when it fixed the R2b rule. The Phase-2b seeds 0-2 serve instead as a coarse check of the training path (Sec. 6). The two arms of a seed start from the same weights and visit the instances in the same order: S changes the values of the input features, not their number, so both arms build the same network from the seed (a test checks it). The selection on the instances remains: R2b reads the validation split and fine set on which 4e found the deficit and R2a was adjudicated, and S's gain should be largest where the deficit is; this favours support, as PREREG_W9 Sec. 6 states for H2.

Seeds: three per arm, as R2a and as PREREG_FINE3D's R2b rule assumed. Five per arm (ten runs: four more, about 110 h) would raise the support rate wherever S shrinks the growth, but lower the expected value at every effect Sec. 5 simulates (Sec. 5).

The factor 2^-10 is R2a's (`scripts/make_fine3d_configs.py`, `S3_FACTOR`; PREREG_FINE3D Sec. 2: the power of two nearest the median of max|F| / sum|F| on 256 fresh instances of the training family, 0.00113). The transformer's default decode scale is the same largest nodal force as the bottleneck's, so at training mesh sizes S asks it for outputs of about the Phase-2b level on the median instance (1.16 times). S changes the decode scale and the load inputs together, and R2b tests them together; a SUPPORTED R2b is a statement about S as a whole.

The code: no source change. The transformer takes `decode_scale`, `decode_scale_factor` and `features.load_density` since wp9 (PREREG_W9, in two dimensions) and the three-dimensional load densities (traction per boundary face area, body load per volume) since fine3d (PREREG_FINE3D Sec. 2); with the defaults it is the Phase-2b transformer bit for bit on CPU (the cross-branch regression against `wp8-lejepa` on the Phase-2b configuration, `records/wp9/regression_wp8_phase2b.json`, run on this source tree). P3 takes E8's seed offset (fine3d): its zero-shot rows read the states E8 trained (`ar_p1024_s3/4/5.pt`) and record those seeds.

## 3. Evaluation sets and economy
- **In band:** the validation split of E2 and Phase-2b (256 instances of `runs/data3d_phase2`, split seed 1). **Fine:** P3's fine evaluation set (the first 256 instances of `runs/data3d_phase2_fine` in manifest order). Both are labelled since Phase 2; neither run buys a label (the solve ledgers read 0).
- Neither run reads the in-band corpus's tail (from which R1's holdout is drawn) or the fine set's 64-instance few-shot prefix.
- Three of the fine set's instances (212, 224 and 232) have the geometry, material and load scales of instances of the in-band training pool (198, 210 and 218), which the networks saw at the in-band mesh size (cmame-paper's `records/cmame/holdout/overlap.json`, at 37b3afe); every comparison here reads them in both arms alike.

## 4. Measurements
Per seed: E8's AR cell on the validation split; P3's zero-shot pass on the fine set and again on the validation split; the frozen metric suite per instance (relative energy gap, displacement relative L2, von Mises relative L2, peak von Mises error, critical-region recall). Per-seed values are means over a set's instances; arm values are seed means. The fine / in-band ratio of a seed is P3's fine mean over P3's in-band mean (both from P3's pass, as KP4 reads it); the condition of Sec. 5 reads the mean of the per-seed ratios; the ratio of the seed means is reported beside it. K1 reads E8's AR cell (as PREREG_E2's K1 and R2a do). After each run, `scripts/posthoc_amplitude.py` (wp8 4e, `--remesh 16`) on it: c* per instance and load case on both sets, the displacement error and energy gap after scaling by c*, and the remesh pass -- 16 geometries, each meshed at lc 0.0906, 0.0742, 0.0579 and 0.0374 -- with c_b and the predicted energy norm relative to the coarsest mesh. Secondary readings only.

## 5. Hypothesis and verdict
Noise guard (the E-series rule, PREREG_E1 r13): for arm values A (new) and B (reference) from three seeds each, rel = mean(A)/mean(B) - 1 and SE_rel = sqrt(s_A^2/3 + s_B^2/3) / mean(B) with sample standard deviations (n - 1); tau = max(10%, 2 x SE_rel). "Lower beyond the guard" means rel < -tau; "worse beyond the guard" means rel > tau. The tau applied is reported with each verdict, as are the per-seed values.
- **R2b (primary).** Reference: `r2b_b`. **SUPPORTED** iff all of: (i) S's fine displacement error is lower beyond the guard; (ii) S's fine / in-band displacement-error ratio is lower beyond the guard (the growth itself shrinks, not only both levels); (iii) K1, read on E8's in-band cell: neither S's energy gap nor its displacement error there is worse beyond the guard. Otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED**, with the failing conditions named. This is R2a's rule (PREREG_FINE3D Sec. 6), unchanged, on the transformer. A non-finite per-seed value of S fails the condition it enters (reported as diverged); a non-finite value of the fresh baseline voids the comparison (R2b not evaluated; its readings and deviations are still reported); a seed mean non-finite in only one of P3's in-band pass and E8's cell is refused instead (Sec. 7).
- Properties by simulation (`scripts/r2b_sims.py`, `records/wp9/r2b/simulations.json`: 200,000 trials per case, three log-normal seed values per arm, the spreads of the Phase-2b transformer -- coefficients of variation 17% for the fine displacement error, 9% and 25% for the in-band displacement error and energy gap -- for both arms, a seed's values drawn independently, unless stated): a support without a true effect occurs in 2.7% of trials, and one without a true change of the growth -- S lowering the fine and in-band errors alike, by 10% / 30% / 50% / 70% -- in 4.8% / 5.5% / 5.6% / 5.7%. With the in-band errors unchanged, a true fine reduction of 30% / 50% / 70% / 90% is supported in 49% / 87% / 90% / 90% of trials; the rate stays near 90% however large the effect, because K1 reads two in-band metrics at the guard's resolution and fires on noise in about one trial in ten. If S lowers the in-band errors too, the fine error must fall by more than they do, since condition (ii) asks the growth itself to shrink: with the in-band errors 30% lower, a fine reduction of 50% / 70% is supported in 56% / 99.3% of trials, and with them 50% lower, one of 50% / 70% / 80% in 5.6% / 84% / 99.7%. If S raises them by 10%, K1 fires more often, and a fine reduction of 50% / 70% is supported in 62% / 63%. Drawn independently, a seed's fine and in-band errors spread its ratio by about 20%, while the Phase-2b seeds' ratios spread by 9% (9.01 / 9.00 / 7.73): the rates that turn on condition (ii) are, if anything, low (a correlation within a seed raises them; below).
- At the effects the records suggest (`anchors`; each a fine reduction with in-band changes of its own): what c* does to the Phase-2b states (the fine error -68%, the in-band displacement error -53% and energy gap -19%) is supported in 65% of trials, condition (ii) failing in nearly all the rest (84% with a correlation of 0.5 within a seed between its fine and in-band errors, which three seeds cannot estimate; 47% with the spreads drawn in every trial from their uncertainty given three seeds, at most 4 times their estimates); the same fine reduction with the in-band errors unchanged in 90%; what S did to the bottleneck (-94%, in band -76% and -72%) in 100% (94% with the spreads drawn); 0.87 times each of those three changes (fine -82%, in band -66% and -63%; 0.87 is the ratio of c*'s fine reductions on the transformer and the bottleneck, 68% and 78%) in 94% (70% with the spreads drawn, 49% with both arms' spreads twice their estimates); half of S's effect on the bottleneck in 23%; c* on the bottleneck, for reference, in 68%. R2b's support turns on how much S also lowers the transformer's in-band errors: with its fine error 0.87 times S's on the bottleneck (-82%) but its in-band errors lowered as the bottleneck's were (-76% and -72%), the support falls to 45% (34% with the spreads drawn, 63% with the correlation of 0.5). A NOT SUPPORTED R2b with an observed fine reduction of 50-80% is therefore weak evidence against S; one that fails condition (ii) alone is reported as such (Sec. 6): the fine error lower beyond the guard, the in-band errors not worse, the growth not shown to shrink.
- Seeds. On five seeds per arm the support rises wherever S shrinks the growth (c* on the Phase-2b states: 65% to 84%; 0.87 times S's effect on the bottleneck: 94% to 99.6%; the case of the lower fine reduction alone: 45% to 61%) and falls where it does not (the fine and in-band errors both 30% lower: 5.5% to 3.9%), but with a supported R2b worth 1.5, 2 or 3 times the cost of six runs and a NOT SUPPORTED one nothing (PREREG_FINE3D Sec. 6's convention), the expected value of five seeds is below that of three at each of the seven effects of `anchors`, with the spreads at their estimates and drawn (`value`): R2b runs three seeds per arm.
- **What the verdict licenses.** SUPPORTED: on these seeds and instances, at this budget, model and training family, S's transformer has a fine displacement error and a fine / in-band ratio lower than the fresh baseline's beyond tau, and in-band errors not worse beyond it. NOT SUPPORTED: no such reduction was shown; it is not evidence that the effect is absent or small. A reduction smaller than tau cannot be shown by design, and one near tau is missed about half the time. No verdict is restated as "no effect"; each condition is reported with rel, SE_rel, tau and the per-seed values. R2b is a statement about the paper's transformer at one fine mesh size (lc 0.0374) of its training family; R2a and R2b read different networks and are separate claims.

## 6. Secondary readings (reported, no verdict; exploratory)
- Beside each condition: the same guard on per-seed medians over the instances (for the ratio, the per-seed ratio of the medians); rel with a Welch 95% interval; a 95% interval for rel from resampling the instances (2,000 resamples, the same instances for both arms and every seed, the seeds kept). Per seed, the paired relative difference between the arms for each condition's metric (the arms of a seed share its initial weights and instance order; the guard treats them as independent).
- Every metric on the fine set, on P3's in-band pass and on E8's cell, S against the fresh baseline with the guard computed: the fine energy gap, von Mises error, peak von Mises error and critical-region recall among them (the fine energy gap of the Phase-2b states spread by 72% across seeds, 0.530 / 0.296 / 0.091, so its guard is wide).
- Mechanism, from the post-hoc records: the median c* on both sets (near 1 on the fine set if S removes the deficit), the errors after scaling by c*, and on the remesh geometries, by lc, the predicted energy norm relative to the coarsest mesh (the Phase-2b states' fell to 0.75 at lc 0.0374 in wp8 4e; S's should not fall) and c_b (theirs rose from 0.98 at lc 0.0906 to 1.27 at 0.0374; S's should stay near 1). Beyond the amplitude: S's fine displacement error against the fresh baseline's after scaling by c* (per seed, with the guard): whether S does more than correct the amplitude (on the bottleneck it did: 0.0310 against 0.122).
- Beside condition (ii): each arm's fine / in-band ratio of seed means against KP4's line of 1.5 (PREREG_PHASE2B; the Phase-2b transformer's 8.62 retired its transfer zone): whether S brings the transformer's growth below it, as it brought the bottleneck's (1.20 against 4.79, the ratios of the seed means).
- If condition (ii) alone fails, the verdict file says so in words: S's fine displacement error lower beyond the guard and its in-band errors not worse, but the growth not shown to shrink.
- If the fresh baseline does not show the deficit -- its fine / in-band ratio of seed means below 1.5 or its median c* on the fine set below 1.10, PREREG_W9's rule-1 lines -- a NOT SUPPORTED R2b is reported as uninformative about S's mechanism; the verdict stands (without the baseline's amplitude record, or when its median c* on the fine set is not finite, the c* part is reported as not evaluated).
- The fresh baseline beside the Phase-2b seeds 0-2 (the fine displacement error, E8's in-band energy gap and displacement error, with the guard): a difference beyond the guard is reported as a flag on the training path and changes nothing.
- R2a beside R2b: each condition's rel and tau on the bottleneck (`records/wp9/fine3d/fine3d_verdict.json`) beside the transformer's, and the ratio of S's fine reductions on the two (Sec. 5's 0.87, as measured).
- Everything per seed as well as seed-averaged.

## 7. Execution mechanics (exact)
- **Stamping**, before any run: `scripts/stamp_prereg_r2b.py --date "<date>"` fills the two CONFIG_SHA256 lines (each the canonical SHA-256 of its configuration, as `fejepa prereg --stamp` computes it), the status line and the last line, this file's SHA-256, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_R2B_SHA256 = <record after commit>` (a file cannot contain its own hash). It refuses a file that is not this draft byte for byte, configurations other than the generator's, and a `src` that is not the tree the cross-branch regression ran (Sec. 8). Then commit; run the full suite on that commit; tag it `prereg-r2b` (an annotated tag); push the branch and the tag; the remote tag is verified before any GPU time. The branch is frozen until the session has returned: before the session and before every step, the box checks that `git describe --always --dirty --tags` -- the string every report records -- prints exactly `prereg-r2b` and that it is the only tag on the commit.
- **The session** (`RUNBOOK_R2B.md`; `scripts/r2b_session.sh` runs the steps in this order and keeps every attempt's log): `run-config configs/r2b_s.json`, the post-hoc amplitude pass on its report, `run-config configs/r2b_b.json`, the post-hoc amplitude pass on its report -- S first, so that the new path (S on the three-dimensional transformer) fails, if it fails, before the baseline's 83 h are spent: the baseline starts only once S's report is complete (R2b needs both arms; the status file records the check). The order cannot otherwise favour either arm (the arms run as separate processes and nothing either reads depends on the other). Each step's start in the status file records the host and GPU driver it starts on, read with its time before the line is written. Every training step is guard-verified against this file: a run-config refuses to start unless its configuration's hash is its own line here; the post-hoc passes read those guard-verified reports and the states they record. A step whose output is complete is skipped when the script runs again; a run with states but no report restarts with `--reuse-states` (D9; recorded in its report: finished seeds reused, an interrupted one resumed from its epoch checkpoint). Before every step it runs, the session also checks that the GPU is there, works (an allocation and a synchronisation) and is free (90% of its memory): a step without it is not started, and the status file says why. A check of a step's output that itself fails (killed, out of memory, an I/O error) moves and runs nothing, and the status file says so before that step. Ctrl-C stops the session once the command it interrupts has ended, with a status line saying so. An epoch checkpoint or a D9 state whose file cannot be read is moved aside and trained again; one that reads but cannot be restored stops the step and is kept. A remesh geometry that gmsh cannot mesh is recorded and the pass goes on; an error while the states are evaluated stops the pass. (The same mechanics as PREREG_FINE3D Sec. 8's, which the box ran.)
- **Adjudication:** `scripts/adjudicate_r2b.py`, from the return. It refuses an R2b report that is not a guard-verified run of this file under its own line, whose configuration does not hash to its recorded SHA-256, that ran on another commit than `prereg-r2b`, differs from the Phase-2b configuration beyond the keys of Sec. 2 or is not the stamped arm for its role, ran on another corpus or other seeds, or does not hold one evaluation per seed with a value for each of the set's instances (E8's and P3's cells), or whose P3 in-band pass does not reproduce its E8 AR cell (the seed means of the displacement error and energy gap to a relative 1e-3: the same states on the same split; a seed mean finite in one of the two only fails, and one finite in neither, a diverged seed, is left to Sec. 5); and it refuses inputs whose SHA-256 is not the records' (the Phase-2b report, fine3d's verdict file). A report that is missing from the return or cannot be read leaves R2b NOT EVALUATED. A post-hoc amplitude record of another report, or an incomplete one (a seed missing, or a seed's in-band or fine pass unfinished or its reproduction unchecked), is not read (its readings are not evaluated); one whose remesh pass is short of its 16 geometries or has a failed one is read without its remesh readings, a deviation; one whose predictions differ from the report's by more than 5% (relative, per instance, an instance non-finite in both not compared) is read with a deviation. Restarts, resumed seeds, repeated attempts, steps not started, steps whose last attempt did not exit 0, a step the session does not have, the baseline started before the session found S's report complete after S's last attempt, steps started on more than one host or GPU driver, and a start whose host is not recorded or whose GPU driver could not be read (from the session's status file; a status file not given or not in the return is itself one), a solve ledger above 0, and a different torch, GPU or TF32 setting between the two arms are recorded as deviations. The verdict file records the adjudicating code -- its `git describe` and its files' SHA-256 -- and whether those files and the pre-registration it read are the tag's; a difference, or a tag that cannot be read there, is a deviation.
