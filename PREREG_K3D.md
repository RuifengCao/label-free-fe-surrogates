# PRE-REGISTRATION K3D -- the three-dimensional supervised transformer retrained with the stiffness-norm loss

**Branch:** cmame-paper. **Status:** r1, stamped 21 October 2026 before the run: the CONFIG_SHA256 line below is filled, and the last line is this file's SHA-256 (Sec. 6); post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** every outcome below is publishable; any revision of the manuscript (`paper/cmame/`) reports this run's verdicts and reading whatever they are.

## 1. Questions
Background. PREREG_CM2D tested, in two dimensions, whether training the supervised transformer with the stiffness norm of the error (L_K) in place of the relative Euclidean displacement error (L_D), changing nothing else, lowers its relative energy gap and its von Mises error at 1,024 labelled instances. Both reductions were shown (`records/cmame/cm2d/verdict.json`): H2a, seed means of the relative energy gap 0.0290 against 0.1334, rel = -78.2% with tau = 16.4%; H2b, von Mises error 0.106 against 0.235, -54.9% with tau = 12.3%. The stiffness-norm transformer's relative energy gap was not shown to differ from the label-free transformer's (H3: -24.2%, tau = 38.1%). The manuscript's three-dimensional comparison (Phase-2b, `records/wp8/e2/baseline/report_phase2b.json`, "Phase-2b" below) has the label-free transformer (the code's amortised-Ritz regime, AR) and the supervised transformer trained with L_D on the same 1,024 instances, but no stiffness-norm control: the manuscript's explanation of the label-free network's lower stress errors by the norm of its objective has been tested in two dimensions only. This run adds the control in three dimensions.

On Phase-2b's 256 validation instances the label-free transformer's seed means are 0.0104 / 0.0105 / 0.0065 in the relative energy gap (mean 0.0091) and 0.0587 / 0.0574 / 0.0503 in the relative L2 von Mises error (0.0554); the L_D transformer's are 0.297 / 92.99 / 0.365 (31.2) and 0.272 / 0.530 / 0.307 (0.370). One validation instance carries L_D's seed means: instance 107 of the split (`instance_00485.npz`), on which seed 1 has a relative energy gap of 23,727 and a von Mises error of 65.0; without it that seed's means are 0.306 and 0.277. The per-seed medians over the 256 instances are 0.224 / 0.256 / 0.275 for L_D's relative energy gap (coefficient of variation, sample standard deviation over mean, 10.2%), 0.247 / 0.254 / 0.276 for its von Mises error (5.9%), and 0.0093 / 0.0074 / 0.0058 for the label-free transformer's relative energy gap (23.3%). On 18 of the 768 instance-seed pairs L_D's relative energy gap is above 1 (a prediction further from the solution, in the energy norm, than the zero field), on none of the label-free transformer's. Phase-2b also has a labels+energy row (L_D plus the energy, gradient-balanced; its states were not kept): seed means 0.139 / 0.0587 / 0.149 in the relative energy gap and 0.135 / 0.119 / 0.132 in the von Mises error.

Notation as in PREREG_CM2D Sec. 1. An instance has a stiffness matrix K (assembled, symmetric positive definite on the free degrees of freedom) and L = 4 load vectors F_j with reference solutions U*_j; ||v||_K = (v^T K v)^(1/2) over the free degrees of freedom; a network predicts u_j, zero on the constrained ones; the relative energy gap is g_j = ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 / ||U*_j||_K^2. L_D = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_2 / ||U*_j||_2, and L_K = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_K / ||U*_j||_K = (1/L) sum_j g_j^(1/2) (`SupervisedConfig.loss = "knorm"`): the same loss with the norm replaced and nothing else. The label-free objective is (1/L) sum_j [u_j^T K u_j / 2 - F_j^T u_j] = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 / 2 + const: the same norm, squared and not normalised.

- **Q2a-3D, Q2b-3D.** In three dimensions, does training the supervised transformer with L_K instead of L_D, changing nothing else, lower (a) its relative energy gap and (b) its von Mises error at 1,024 labels, on the typical validation instance (per-seed medians, Sec. 4)? (a) is close to a check that the change of loss took effect, since L_K minimises the mean over load cases of g_j^(1/2) on the training instances; (b), the von Mises error, is a term of neither loss -- by the manuscript's Proposition "Energy gap and stress error" the stiffness norm of the error is its volume-integrated stress-error energy and bounds the volume-weighted von Mises error, so L_K penalises that bound and L_D does not -- and reads the element-wise error of the evaluation suite; it is the test of the manuscript's explanation in three dimensions.
- **Q3-3D.** How does the transformer trained with L_K compare with the label-free one, which trained on the same 1,024 instances? The two objectives weight the same residual differently per load case and were trained with different learning rates; a two-sided reading, not a test.

## 4. Hypotheses and verdicts
Noise guard (the E-series rule, as in PREREG_CM2D Sec. 4), on the per-seed medians of Sec. 3: for a new arm A and a reference B with three per-seed values each, rel = mean(A)/mean(B) - 1 and SE_rel = sqrt(s_A^2/3 + s_B^2/3) / mean(B) with sample standard deviations (n - 1); tau = max(10%, 2 x SE_rel). "Lower beyond the guard" means rel < -tau; "worse beyond the guard" means rel > tau. Each verdict is reported with rel, SE_rel, tau and the per-seed values; a NOT SUPPORTED verdict is reported as "worse" when A is worse beyond the guard. A non-finite per-seed value of B in the metric the hypothesis reads, or a mean of B that is not positive, leaves the hypothesis NOT EVALUATED, reported with the per-seed values (B is checked first, so also when A has a non-finite value too); a non-finite per-seed value of A alone is a divergence, NOT SUPPORTED (diverged), reported without rel or tau. A per-seed value above 1e150 counts as non-finite (the guard's exactly rounded arithmetic overflows near 1e154).

Why medians. PREREG_CM2D read seed means. Here one instance carries the reference's seed means (Sec. 1): with them, SE_rel of the L_D reference alone is 0.989 in the relative energy gap, so tau >= 1.98, and since rel >= -1 a reduction could never be shown, whatever L_K does; in the von Mises error tau >= 0.437. A median of 256 values moves by at most one rank when any one value changes, however far. The guard on per-seed medians is PREREG_CM2D's pre-registered robustness reading (its Sec. 4), whose two-dimensional values were known when it was chosen here: -83.3% (tau 11.6%) for H2a, -53.6% (11.8%) for H2b and -21.4% (33.7%) for H3, in the directions of the verdicts and of the reading. Other robust statistics of the reference spread alike across its seeds: 10%-trimmed means 11.0% and 6.2%, geometric means 10.8% and 5.9%, seed means without instance 107 11.1% and 6.4% (relative energy gap and von Mises error), against the medians' 10.2% and 5.9%. Had one instance not carried the seed means, PREREG_CM2D's statistic would have been kept. The statistic was chosen after the reference's per-instance values were known and before any network was trained with L_K in three dimensions; the seed-mean reading is reported beside every verdict (no criterion). Instance 107 is among the three largest relative energy gaps of every stored row and seed (label-free 0.035 / 0.087 / 0.021, labels+energy 0.27 / 1.97 / 0.68, graph network 27 / 3.3 / 55), so it may well carry the L_K transformer's seed means too.

## 6. Execution mechanics (exact)
- Stamping, after approval: `scripts/stamp_prereg_k3d.py --date "<D Month YYYY>"` refuses a file whose status line, CONFIG_SHA256 line or last line is not this r1 draft's, and a configuration that fails `scripts/k3d_train.py`'s own checks apart from the guard (its keys; the source report by SHA-256; the training block against the trainer's defaults; the source report's embedded configuration by its own hash, float32 without compilation, E8's budget, epochs and learning rate, the seeds; the stored states' SHA-256 against the records they are copied from; the holdout block's form); it fills CONFIG_SHA256[k3d_v1] with the configuration's canonical SHA-256 (as `fejepa prereg --stamp`), the status line with the date, and the last line with this file's SHA-256, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_K3D_SHA256 = <record after commit>`; then it reads the result back and verifies it. Commit; tag `prereg-k3d` (no other tag on that commit); push the branch and the tag; the remote tag is verified before the run. The branch is frozen until the run has returned.
- The run (`RUNBOOK_CMAME.md` Sec. F), with nothing else on the GPU and after PREREG_HOLDOUT's run (whose labels it reads). Before it: the checkout is `prereg-k3d` (`git describe --always --dirty --tags` prints exactly that, which the script also requires of a guarded run and the report records) without local changes; the suite passes; the holdout labels manifest on the box is the one PREREG_HOLDOUT's return holds (by SHA-256); the container's memory limit is at least 64 GB; the dry run (`--dry-run`) verifies this file's stamp (its CONFIG_SHA256 line and its last line), the source report, the corpus manifest and the training instances' labels, the stored states, and the holdout labels manifest and files, and plans 1,024 training and 256 validation instances and 686 holdout instances; no earlier attempt exists under `runs/k3d/`. Then one `python scripts/k3d_train.py configs/k3d_v1.json`. Before anything runs on the GPU, the script requires a GPU that works (an allocation and a synchronisation) and is free (90% of its memory); it never falls back to the CPU (exit status 2). It evaluates the six stored states and stops before any training if one fails its content check (exit status 5); then it trains the seeds in order 0, 1, 2, writing an epoch checkpoint at most every 5 minutes; then it evaluates the holdout set; then it writes the report. A restart after an interruption runs the same command: the stored states' evaluations and the finished seeds of an earlier attempt of this code and configuration are kept, an interrupted seed resumes from its epoch checkpoint if this code, configuration and seed wrote it (else it trains from the start), and the report records each of these. If the holdout evaluation fails in two consecutive attempts once every seed has trained, the same command with `--skip-holdout` writes the report without the holdout set (RUNBOOK_CMAME Sec. F names the other failures that the same command runs again once; a failure of that attempt, or of the `--skip-holdout` run, is reported and decided on, not retried by rule). One run at a time: the script holds a lock while it runs. If the stored states do not reproduce Phase-2b's values (exit status 5), nothing is trained and no verdict is made; the revision of the manuscript then reports that the control could not be run, and why.
- Economy: the run solves nothing (its training instances carry Phase-2's labels; the holdout labels are PREREG_HOLDOUT's).

## 9. What this run does not test
- L_K uses L_D's settings (learning rate, schedule, epochs, gradient clipping), not tuned for it: a NOT SUPPORTED H2a-3D or H2b-3D does not show that no stiffness-norm loss would do better. "Changing nothing else" holds for the settings, not for how they act: at the same predictions L_K's value is several times L_D's (g_j^(1/2) against a relative Euclidean error), so the same gradient-norm clipping and AdamW act on gradients of another scale.
- The verdicts read per-seed medians, a statistic chosen after the reference's per-instance values were seen (Sec. 4, "Why medians"); the seed means, which one instance carries in the reference, are reported beside them with no criterion. CM2D's verdicts read seed means: the two-dimensional result to set beside these verdicts is CM2D's median reading. A SUPPORTED verdict says nothing about the hardest instances (instance 107, the pairs worse than the zero field), which Sec. 5 reports. H2b-3D tests whether the stiffness norm lowers the von Mises error, not how much of the label-free transformer's advantage it accounts for (Sec. 5 reports the share it closes, descriptively). H2b-3D uses the element-wise relative L2 von Mises error of the evaluation suite, not the volume-weighted error that the manuscript's Proposition "Energy gap and stress error" bounds.
- The L_K and L_D transformers were not trained side by side: L_D on the box in September 2026 with the code of then, L_K now with this code, whose training path the sandbox regression shows equal to then on CPU miniatures. GPU training is not bitwise reproducible, and the box's host may have changed; the stored states are evaluated again by this run, with this code, and must reproduce Phase-2b's values.
- The decisions read the 256 validation instances on which the L_D and label-free values of Sec. 1 were observed; the holdout reading adds 686 instances on which no network trained, as a reading only.
- H3-3D compares two weightings of the same residual at two learning rates (label-free 1e-3, L_K 1.5e-3), not one ingredient.
- One corpus, one model size, one budget, three dimensions, no out-of-distribution set.
