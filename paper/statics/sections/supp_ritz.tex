\section{The Ritz projection paired with the networks}\label{supp:ritz}

The paper pairs the polynomial Ritz-Galerkin projection of each instance
(\cref{sec:methods:ritz}), computed from the instance's own stiffness matrix and loads
without labels or training, with every network's prediction of the same instance; this
baseline was added after the pre-registered runs and was not pre-registered.
\Cref{tab:ritzpairs} gives, for each set, model and degree $p$ of the ladder, the share of
the instances on which the projection was strictly the more accurate, as the lowest and the
highest over the three seeds of a trained network. The sets are the validation instances of
the three- and two-dimensional comparisons, their held-out instances, and the finer
three-dimensional mesh, on which the networks trained in-band were applied zero-shot. The
projection has \numRitzThreeK{} unknowns at $p=6$, 7, 8 and 9 in three dimensions and
\numRitzTwoK{} at $p=6$ and 8 in two. It counts as the more accurate where its error is
lower, or its critical-region recall higher; a tie, which occurs only in critical-region
recall, counts for neither side, and a share strictly between none and all of the instances
is never rounded to either. The networks are those trained with 1,024 labelled instances;
on the finer mesh the few-shot networks are those of \cref{tab:transfer}. The shares for the
other networks and label budgets are in the records.
