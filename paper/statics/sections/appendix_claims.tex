\Cref{tab:claims} lists the pre-registered claims of the three comparisons of this paper
(\cref{sec:methods:prereg}), and the pre-registered test of a warm start, with the values measured and the outcomes as the code recorded them at the time.
\Cref{tab:holdout} gives
those of the holdout evaluation. The hypotheses of the comparisons are evaluated in
\cref{sec:norm,sec:labelfree}, and the gate of the three-dimensional comparison in
\cref{sec:labelfree:three}. Supplementary Section~\SuppPrereg{} lists every criterion of every
run, including the earlier runs of July 2026, with the departures from the plans.

The criteria and their numbers are those of the pre-registration documents. Gates
decide whether a study proceeds to its next stage; kill conditions withdraw a claim when
triggered. In the gate G2 of the three-dimensional comparison, (a) is a plausibility check of
the supervised side, (b) the accuracy of the label-free network and (c) its transfer to the finer mesh. The amendment of the gate, written after the initial
run, assessed (a) from 64 labels only; the naive baselines of (a) are the nearest-neighbour field and the scaled
polynomial of \cref{sec:methods:networks}. In the statements, $\mathcal{L}_D$ and
$\mathcal{L}_K$ are the losses \eqref{eq:ld} and \eqref{eq:lk}.

The noise guard compares a network with a comparator network over three seeds each, through
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathrm{rel}&=\frac{\mathrm{mean}(\text{network})}{\mathrm{mean}(\text{comparator})}-1,\\
\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{rel}}&=\frac{\bigl(\mathrm{sd}(\text{network})^2/3
+\mathrm{sd}(\text{comparator})^2/3\bigr)^{1/2}}{\mathrm{mean}(\text{comparator})},
\end{split}
\label{eq:guard}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{mean}$ and $\mathrm{sd}$ are the mean and the sample standard deviation of the metric over a network's seeds. The network is lower beyond the guard when $\mathrm{rel}$ is below minus the guard,
$\max(10\%,\,2\,\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{rel}})$, and higher beyond it when $\mathrm{rel}$
exceeds the guard; within it no difference is shown, which is not equivalence. The
three-dimensional energy-norm run applies it to each seed's median over the instances.
